Pro Bono

Pro bono MEL support for local and national organizations

From early 2027, I'm offering one organization at a time 8–12 weeks of senior, remote Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) support, at no cost to your organization.

I specialize in emergency response and quick-turnaround monitoring. I've spent 13+ years designing indicators, data systems and donor reporting for humanitarian programs in Ukraine, Yemen, Lebanon, Nepal, Uganda and 10+ other countries. The goal is a system your own staff can run after I hand it over.

What I need from you

  • A registered organization with a bank account in the organization's name
  • One staff member who can give about 2 hours a week and will own the system after handover
  • A real use for the work, such as a donor report, a proposal or a program decision
  • Reliable internet for a weekly video call

How it works

  1. Week 1: a 45-minute call to agree on the package, what you'll get and the timeline
  2. Weeks 2–10: I build the work with your staff member, with a weekly 1-hour call
  3. Final week: handover and a short training, so your team can run it without me

In return, I ask only for permission to describe the work, without sensitive details, in my portfolio, and for a short reference at the end.

How to apply

Email a short description of your organization, the package you want, and the decision or report it will feed into.
